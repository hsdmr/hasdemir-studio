Privacy Policy

Stellar Drop · Hasdemir Studio · Effective date: 24 November 2026

EnglishTürkçe

1. Who we are

Stellar Drop ("the Game") is developed by Hasdemir Studio, an independent developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles and why.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers that receive data from the Game.

3. Data stored on your device

Your progress (scores, stardust, discovered planets, achievements, unlocked themes, missions, unfinished games, settings, and whether the rating and shop reminders have been shown) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data. The "More games" screen downloads a list of our other games from our website (hasdemir.tr); no personal data is sent, and the list and icons are kept on your device. The "Rate us" button and the game cards only open a Google Play page in your browser or the Play Store app; they send nothing from the Game.

4. Advertising

The Game may show ads, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, approximate location (estimated from your IP address), how you interact with ads, and crash and diagnostic information about the ad service. Google uses this information according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the device).

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional purchases ("Remove ads" and "Support the developer"). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; the Game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was completed, which it uses to unlock the item on your device. Google processes payment information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use the controls described in Google's policies. If you have a question or request, contact us and we will help.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.

9. Contact

Hasdemir Studio · hsdmrsoft@gmail.com

Oyun için hesap oluşturmanız gerekmez. Adınızı, e-posta adresinizi, telefon numaranızı, rehberinizi, fotoğraflarınızı, kesin konumunuzu veya başka bir kişisel verinizi toplamayız. Oyun'dan veri alan kendi sunucularımız yoktur.

İlerlemeniz (skorlar, yıldız tozu, keşfedilen gezegenler, başarımlar, açılan temalar, görevler, yarım kalan oyunlar, ayarlar ve değerlendirme ile market hatırlatmalarının gösterilip gösterilmediği) yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır ve bize gönderilmez. Oyun'u kaldırdığınızda bu veriler silinir.
