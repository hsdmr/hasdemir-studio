Puzzle · Android

Lotus Stack

Place stacks of colorful leaves onto a hexagon garden. Leaves of the same color join together, and ten of one color open like a lotus and bloom away. Calm, tidy and full of small decisions.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Place, match, bloomDrag a stack onto an empty hex. Only the top color joins its neighbors, so every move counts. Gather ten of a color to open a lotus.
  • Chain bloomsLeaves slide from stack to stack. Open several lotuses in one move for a chain multiplier.
  • Classic, Goals and DailyEndless Classic with growing colors, endless Goals levels with move limits and a new Daily puzzle every day with a streak counter.
  • Soft soundsEvery color has its own gentle note. No timers and no loud effects.
  • Mix your gardenA market of 30 styles: 10 stone looks, 10 board looks and 10 ambiences such as Zen, Chakra, Sakura, Bamboo, Fireflies and Snow. Plus daily tasks, a login streak and thirteen achievements.
  • Your round is savedClose the game any time and continue exactly where you left off, in every mode. Plays offline.
  • More from the studioA built-in "More games" screen shows our other games. English and Turkish, optional rewarded ads and no pushy pop-ups.
